Our story

A garden that grew
into a gathering place.

Tucked into the quiet lanes of Shahpura, Thevan Garden Diaries began as a patch of green and a handful of chairs under the trees. What stayed the same as it grew is the feeling of arriving somewhere unhurried — where the branches do the decorating, the light changes the mood through the day, and nobody is in a rush to hand you the bill.

It's less a restaurant with a garden, and more a garden that happens to serve very good food. Every table finds its own pocket of shade, its own view, its own quiet.

The experience

Four ways to lose track of time.

Garden Dining

Tables scattered under old trees, canopies of leaves standing in for a ceiling, and just enough distance between one conversation and the next.

Food & Beverages

Slow food, seasonal ingredients and a menu that changes with what the garden and the market are willing to give up that day.

Relaxed Evenings

As the light softens, the lanterns take over — long dinners, low music, and no particular reason to check the time.

Beautiful Moments

Anniversaries, quiet mornings, long-overdue catch-ups — the garden has a way of making all of them feel a little more special.
